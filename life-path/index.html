What it is

Your path, written in numbers

The Life Path Number is the most important number in numerology.

It reveals the central purpose of your life: the talents you brought with you, the challenges you came to move through, and the deepest direction of your journey. Other numbers describe parts of your personality. The Life Path is the thread that runs through your whole story.

The calculation is ancient, rooted in the idea, defended since Pythagoras, that every number carries a vibration of its own. And it comes from something that belongs only to you: the day you arrived in the world.

How it is calculated

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need my time of birth?

No. The Life Path uses only the day, the month and the year. Your time of birth does not change your number.

How do I receive the reading?

Your reading is delivered as a PDF. After payment, you receive the instructions to get your file.

What are Master Numbers?

They are 11, 22 and 33. They carry a more intense vibration and are not reduced in the calculation. If you have a Master Number, your reading is written specifically for that vibration.

Is the reading different for each person?

Yes. It is written for your number, with your name on the cover and your four Challenge Numbers calculated from your own date of birth.

Can I buy it for someone else?

Of course. Just use the name and date of birth of the person receiving it. It makes a lovely gift for birthdays and new beginnings.

Does numerology predict the future?

Numerology is a tool for self-knowledge. It shows tendencies, talents and challenges, and the choices are always yours.
